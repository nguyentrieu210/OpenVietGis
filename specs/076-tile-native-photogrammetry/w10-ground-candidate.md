# W10 DTM ground candidate
Adds bounded multi-scale directional support filter on minimum cell elevations, slope-aware elevation threshold and at least two low-neighbour votes, nodata preserved, unsupported cells excluded. Integrates into streaming PLY build_dtm via minimum aggregation and coverage gate. This is a heuristic cell-based terrain candidate, not full point classification, CSF/PMF, interpolation, or validated bare-earth DTM. Wide buildings, dense canopy, outliers and steep terrain may require stronger methods and independent ground checks. DSM maximum aggregation unchanged. Two synthetic tests authored, unexecuted.
