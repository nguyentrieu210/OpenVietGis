# Licensing and commerce boundary — technical review, not legal sign-off
The repository root LICENSE is the source of license terms; mobile Rust candidate crates declare AGPL-3.0-or-later. Do not claim that proprietary partner SDK redistribution, dynamic linking, app-store binary distribution or network service obligations are resolved by a commercial entitlement. Track exact dependency versions, notices, source availability, asset licenses and third-party attribution in final release SBOM. Existing marketplace installs and project activations remain authoritative; paid entitlement is a separate scoped permission gate and not a data authorization grant. Core exports remain subject to the underlying data permission, lease and data policy.
No payment processor, checkout, receipt validation, subscription settlement, app-store purchase or refund is implemented. No external payment or secret configuration. Android/iOS store policy and AGPL compatibility need a dated legal/policy review before any commerce rollout. T067 remains unaccepted.
