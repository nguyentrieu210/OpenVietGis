It never accesses internal PostgreSQL/PostGIS credentials directly, never infers CRS (preserving explicit VN-2000 and WGS 84 EPSG metadata), verifies X-Content-SHA256 before loading layers as read-only, and exposes zero write, push, or sync operations.
